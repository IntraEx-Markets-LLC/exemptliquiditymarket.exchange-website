Exempt Liquidity Market

A Vision for Expanding Access to Capital Formation and Secondary Liquidity

Exempt Liquidity Market Exchange is a proposed marketplace initiative of INTRAEX MARKETS LLC, an independent Florida limited liability company, focused on increasing access to investment opportunities, improving liquidity for exempt securities, and creating pathways toward broader participation in public capital markets.

Learn More

Our Mission

America's capital markets thrive when entrepreneurs, investors, and communities have efficient access to capital. Exempt Liquidity Market seeks to support the growth of emerging businesses by facilitating market transparency, secondary liquidity opportunities, and responsible capital formation for issuers utilizing exempt offering frameworks.

Our long-term objective is to help bridge the gap between exempt securities markets and traditional public exchanges while maintaining investor protections, market integrity, and regulatory compliance.

Marketplace Focus

Private Equity

Supporting future liquidity mechanisms for private company ownership interests and private placements.

Exempt Offerings

Exploring opportunities involving securities offered under applicable exemptions from registration.

State Markets

Encouraging capital formation that can benefit local businesses, regional development, and economic growth.

Lowering Barriers to Public Markets

Emerging companies often face significant costs and structural challenges before reaching major public exchanges.

The Exempt Liquidity Market vision is to help create a scalable progression that begins with exempt capital formation, advances through transparent secondary market participation, and ultimately supports companies seeking qualification for broader public market listings.

By improving access to market infrastructure, data visibility, and investor participation, we believe smaller issuers can more effectively pursue long-term growth while preserving confidence in the public markets.
