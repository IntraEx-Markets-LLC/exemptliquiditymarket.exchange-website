# Exempt Liquidity Market Exchange

## Overview

**Exempt Liquidity Market Exchange (ELMX)** is a proposed marketplace initiative operated by **INTRAEX MARKETS LLC**, a Florida limited liability company.

The mission of ELMX is to explore innovative approaches to capital formation, secondary liquidity, and market transparency for exempt and private securities markets while supporting responsible pathways to broader public market participation.

---

## Vision

America's economic growth depends upon efficient access to capital. Small businesses and emerging growth companies often face significant barriers when seeking investment capital and liquidity opportunities.

ELMX seeks to reduce those barriers by supporting:

- Private equity liquidity
- Secondary trading opportunities for exempt securities
- State-registered offering ecosystems
- Community investment initiatives
- Early-stage capital formation
- Issuer readiness for public markets
- Enhanced investor transparency

---

## Long-Term Objectives

### Capital Formation

Promote investment opportunities that support business creation, expansion, and economic growth.

### Liquidity

Explore mechanisms for improving liquidity in exempt and privately issued securities.

### Transparency

Encourage standardized disclosure practices and improved market visibility.

### Market Integrity

Support investor protection and responsible market operations consistent with applicable regulations.

### Public Market Pathway

Provide educational, technological, and operational infrastructure that may assist issuers in preparing for future listing opportunities on national securities exchanges.

---

## Target Markets

ELMX is evaluating marketplace solutions for:

| Market Segment | Description |
